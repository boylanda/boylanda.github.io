I am a Lecturer in Philosophy at the University of Glasgow. My CV is here.

My main interests are in the philosophy of language, philosophical logic and epistemology. I’m currently working mostly on ability modals and indicative conditionals.

I completed my PhD in Philosophy at MIT in 2018. From 2018 to 2020, I was the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation Postdoctoral Associate in the Philosophy Department at Rutgers University, New Brunswick. And from 2020 to 2024, I was an Assistant Professor of Philosophy at Texas Tech University.
