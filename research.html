Evidence and Conditional Propositions

Argues for a new kind of non-factualism about conditionals, on the basis of principles in conditional epistemology.

Persistence is Trivial

Proves new, more general Gibbardian triviality results that illustrate a tension between restrictor and entailment-based theories of the indicative. Provides a semantics that offers the most coherent response to these results.

draft

Conditional Restriction

Shows how to axiomatise the restrictor view of the conditional. Shows that the resulting logic is sound on a variety of semantics and argues for the superiority of the semantics from “Persistence is Trivial”.
